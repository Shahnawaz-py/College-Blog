Day 3 : Business Mela

About

Step into the Innovator’s Arena! Get ready for a power-packed day of creativity, competition, and commerce at Burhani College’s Business Mela! From handmade crafts and mouth-watering food to tech prototypes and smart services, this is where student entrepreneurs turn dreams into reality. Shop. Sell. Inspire. Compete.

RULES:

  • A Team of maximum 3 students are allowed.
  • Maximum 3 teams are allowed from each college.
  • The competition will be held in college auditorium, However online participation is allowed only in case of teams out of Mumbai jurisdication.
  • Judging criteria- Innovation, Display & Marketing Strategies.

Instructions For Stall:

  • All stall owners must maintain decorum and discipline.
  • Stall Registration charges are Rs. 500/.
  • College will provide One table and two chairs only.
  • Decoration and other aspects are on the discretion of the owners.
  • Participants must ensure that they do not use any such objects which will hide or defame or cause trouble to other stalls.
  • Limited food stalls will be allowed on first come first basis.
  • Electricity line will be provided at additional cost.
  • Stall product catalog to be confirmed with the organizing team atleast 15 days prior to the event.
  • The organizing team holds rights to discontinue a stall or may request you to vacate premises on grounds of malpractice.
  • Stalls may have to shift to a different venue in case of rains so please prepare accordingly.
  • Student stalls competing in the event must ensure following criteria :
    a. Good presentation.
    b. Marketing techniques and strategy used.
    c. Product quality and quantity.
