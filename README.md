# NEXTGEN BIZFEST — College Fest Website

NEXTGEN BIZFEST an inter-college business fest organized by the Career Development Cell and Commerce Association.

## About
NEXTGEN BIZFEST encourages students from different colleges to explore:

- Business ideas
- Entrepreneurship
- Innovation
- Business models
- Marketing
- Student-led ventures

The website provides information about all three days of the fest, including event details, rules, timelines, and registration information.

## Features
- Clean and professional landing page
- College fest branding
- Event cards for all three days
- Individual event pages
- Event rules and timelines
- Organizing committee information
- Registration button
- Quick navigation through footer
- Responsive design
- Hover effects and interactive UI

## Events

### Day 1 — Talk Show & Panel Discussion
An interactive session featuring industry leaders, innovators, and visionaries discussing business and entrepreneurship.

### Day 2 — Business Model Competition
Students present their business ideas and models to judges based on innovation, presentation, clarity, and confidence.

### Day 3 — Business Mela
A student entrepreneurship event featuring products, services, food, crafts, technology, and innovative business ideas.
